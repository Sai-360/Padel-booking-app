How it works

A simple booking flow

The user chooses a site, selects a slot and confirms. The backend keeps the final control.

1

Choose your site and court

Sites and courts are loaded from the backend, not hardcoded in the frontend.

2

Select a valid date

Past dates, closed days and dates outside your membership limit are blocked.

3

Confirm and manage

Public matches can be joined by other players. Private matches can receive invited players.
